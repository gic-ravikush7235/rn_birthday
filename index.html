Jab Pehli Baar Dekha...

Kuch cheezein aisi hoti hain jo words mein nahi aati... bas dil pe likh jaati hain, jaise ek gehri si neend mein sapna jo sach ho jaata. Aapko dekhne ke baad kuch aisa hi hua — mann ka ek kone mein ek roshni jag gayi, jo kabhi bujhegi nahi. Aap thi hi alag — aapki aankhen jaise taarein chamakti hain, aapki hasi jaise phoolon ka rang, aapka andaz jaise ek nazarana jo dil ko chhune ko tarasta hai. 🌸💖

Woh Baatein...

Aapki baatein sunna acha lagta hai, jaise ek sukoon bhari raat mein chand ki kiran. Aap jab baat karti hain, honestly, sab kuch simple aur sach lagta hai — dil ki gehraiyon se nikalta ek saaf sa paani. Aapke saath time kaise khatam ho jaata hai — pata hi nahi chalta, jaise ek sapna jo kabhi khatam na ho. Yehi magic hai na aapke andar, jo mujhe har pal mein kho jaane ko majboor karta hai? ✨❤️

Jo Yaad Rahega...

Ye din, ye 17th birthday — ye aapki zindagi ka ek naya chapter hai, jaise ek kitab ka woh pehla panna jo kahaniyon se bhara hota hai. Bahut saare khwaab hain aapke liye — aap unhe poora karengi, mujhe pura yaqeen hai, kyunki aapke andar woh takat hai jo duniya ko badal sakti hai. Aapki ye journey dekhna mujhe sachchi khushi deta hai, jaise ek painter apni painting ko complete hone dekh raha ho. 💫🌟

Ek Chhoti Si Dua...

Krishna aapko hamesha khush rakhe, jaise ek maa apne bachche ko god mein leke sukoon deti hai. Aapki muskaan kabhi kam na ho — woh muskaan jo mere dil ko roshan karta hai, jaise subah ka suraj. Jitna aap deserve karti hain, usse bhi zyada mile aapko — pyaar jo samandar sa ho, khushi jo har lamhe mein bharpoor ho, success jo aapke kadmon mein ho, aur jo bhi aap chahti hain, woh sab aapke liye banaya gaya ho. Happy Birthday, Rani jaan — mere dil ki raani, mere sapnon ki malika. 🎂💝❤️
